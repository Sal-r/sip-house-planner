SIP House Planner: running it on your own computer (Windows)

1. Keep all of the files together in one folder.
2. Double-click Start-Planner.bat.
3. Your browser opens the planner at http://127.0.0.1:8000
4. Leave the "SIP House Planner" command window open while you work.
   Close it when you are done.

The launcher needs Python 3 (Anaconda, Miniconda, or python.org all work).
If it cannot find Python, open Start-Planner.bat in Notepad and follow the
note at the top.

Your work saves automatically in this browser. Use Export Layout to keep a
copy or to move a layout between computers or browsers.

You can also open index.html directly, but it will use the built-in layout
instead of default-layout.json.

Online version: https://sal-r.github.io/sip-house-planner/
